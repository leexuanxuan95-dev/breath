FAQ

Is Wim Hof safe? The technique is well documented but can cause lightheadedness. Never practice in water or while driving. Sit safely and stop if you feel unwell.

Why does Breath need HealthKit? To read your heart rate during sessions and to write Mindful Minutes when you finish. You can decline; the app still works.

How do I cancel? Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Cancel any time.

Restore Purchases? Settings tab → Restore Purchases.
